## Visible failure controls

Inspect neutral start, peak and return before the full export pass. Ground support is measured on actual skin patches, including pelvis/sole/central-palm contacts where applicable. Verify shoulder/hip volume, continuous foot frames, whole-arm/hand-versus-body clearance and natural contact/release; endpoint or nearest-point distances alone miss deformed feet and implausible grasps.5172 must separate crossover arms,1987 must hold the wrist,1986 must support the elbow with the other palm on the upper back,6121 must hug the knee without lifting the seated pelvis, and3204 must keep pelvis and soles on the same support plane through the full upright range.

The shared viewer uses the accepted culling fix for its single custom-skinned character. Diagnostic camera scale is horizontal viewport width, unlike Blender's vertical ortho scale. Captures preserve canvas buffer/display aspect, assert draw calls and nonempty pixels, and retain exact model/viewer/image hashes. Every morph action needs explicit zero/one GLB weights/reset tracks. Partial-source numerical gates and still-image acceptance do not imply global collision freedom.

## Failures observed during this batch

The standalone 5172 diagnostic initially evaluated linear skinning while the saved source and actual export used dual-quaternion skinning. Those early solver results were retired. Diagnostic scripts must assert the active deformation mode and actual desired bone matrices before using surface derivatives. A collision-free pose can still have an unacceptable shoulder silhouette: the 34.43-degree protraction candidate removed measured arm/body crossings but produced a pointed clavicle crest. A generic rest-shape smoothing target did not repair that pose and introduced new axillary crossings; it is not accepted evidence or a reusable correction.

The 1986 hold pose and clearances passed while its entry still contained a 37.638-degree wrist step in one 30fps interval. Native hand-centroid speeds approached 3.43m/s in that segment. Endpoint contact and a broad joint-step threshold therefore did not establish natural movement. The spatial hand route, temporal parameterization, hold pose and surface shape need separate acceptance. Retiming must preserve the source hold recommendation separately from the five-second demonstration.

The 6121 crossed-leg transition initially intersected a hand left resting on the thigh. Clear hands before moving the leg, then acquire the knee and rear-palm supports. Fit the actual central palm and whole hand to the knee surface; a hand centroid or a nearest finger alone can report a misleading grip. Immutable-source elbow and knee hinge frames avoid inherited axial offsets. A local hip-volume component must cover a coherent waist/glute/thigh region while explicitly pinning the actual seated support patch.

Arm-versus-leg clearance is insufficient for a crossed-leg pose. The initial 6121 anchor lay outside the opposite thigh while the actual toes and calf still intersected the other leg. Test both complete leg surfaces across the cycle, using separate limb semantics to distinguish intended same-leg articulation from bilateral penetration. Foot orientation and the knee pole must come from the source anatomical frames before solving a minimum-clearance foot placement.

Independent parallel transport of upper-arm and forearm frames can preserve joint positions while introducing opposite axial rolls at a mixed-weight elbow. In 1986 the bad transition differed from a coherent source hinge plane by approximately 86 and 82 degrees on the two bones. Align the whole articulated chain to a shared anatomical elbow plane, then apply deliberate wrist roll locally. Endpoint positions alone cannot prove a plausible articulated surface.
