## Skinning defects and bounded remedies

The original CC0 body has source waist vertices over 100 mm above the hip with substantial thigh weights. Original plantar vertices also carry shin weights, and several shoulder and posterior-knee boundaries have abrupt binding changes. Pose and frame checks precede any local repair. Keep Basis, rest skeleton, UV, source IDs and topology unchanged; declare every affected binding/target domain and keep its exterior exact.

Do not retain Blender `VertexGroupElement` RNA references while deleting group memberships: mutation can shift the cached elements and leave stale groups. Use stable group names/indices and verify the weights read back from the actual saved body. A normalized solver array alone does not prove that the delivered skin has normalized weights or at most four influences.

The squat's horizontal arms caused excessive shoulder flexion relative to the forward-inclined trunk. Reaching forward 30 degrees below horizontal with modest elbow flexion preserves the source cue and gives a better silhouette. The rejected upper-hip envelope and uncapped local-target probes are not accepted sources. Remaining local self-fold diagnostics must stay distinct from full arm-versus-leg clearance and mandatory game QA.

## Lower-back support contract

The original 45-ID lumbar observation patch includes seven points above the neutral Spine1 head and a recessed centerline groove 1.6–18.94 mm deep. Requiring all 19 midline points within 2 mm of the floor incorrectly combines closing the arch with flattening the original surface profile. Preserve that failed strict-flat diagnostic and all 45 raw coordinates; do not silently turn it into a pass.

The revised contract freezes the original 38 points below the neutral Spine1 head, a neutral bilateral posterior-envelope profile and 12 direct ridge IDs before fitting. It checks profile-adjusted arch gaps with the same 2 / 5 mm illustration tolerances, actual ridge/pelvic contact and full-skin floor bounds. The source-only domain/profile construction is independent of candidate poses. It is not a sourced medical tolerance.

The accepted support foundation must retain the exact original 0003 three compact keys and 151 frame schedule, including declared legacy flank guards. New support corrections are additive and bounded separately. They may be compiled into the same three final target buffers when the schedule is identical, provided an exact component receipt proves final = original + support delta and retains the hashed original inputs; final buffer hashes then intentionally differ. Ancillary knee/elbow/shoulder targets must not silently remove those guards or overlap the frozen support domain.

Passing support measurements did not establish acceptable hip shape. Inspection initially interpreted the long dark thigh edge as a stretched connecting sheet. Subsequent raycasts, neutral source IDs and flat/wire proofs showed that the raised, rigidly bound thigh occludes a separate waist surface 88–199 mm behind it. The earlier continuous-sheet diagnosis was incorrect. Independent visual reassessment accepts this legitimate occlusion with a minor straight low-poly contour and a small lower junction notch. Erasing the contour would merge distinct anatomical surfaces.

A separate local posterior glute binding repair rounds the pointed attachment while preserving the protected 97-vertex support domain and its actual evaluated positions. The attempted additional hip fairing did not materially improve the shape and is excluded from delivery. The bicycle retains its separately accepted additive shoulder correction. Source and transport parity remain distinct from visual judgment; the final GPU review must use these exact components.

## Integration boundaries

Inactive skin stays neutral. Warm and cyan fields are stable artistic load/stretch proxies, not phase-dependent physiological activation. Source labels and overlapping groups remain explicit. One cached squat mesh must restore each exercise's own muscle field and action on selection. Final acceptance requires actual GPU playback, camera fitting, transparency, seeking, touch controls, source/native references and the declared physical gates. Native-only smoothing or a passing metric cannot override a visibly distorted figure.

The original sparse abdominal masks produced conspicuous narrow hot peaks in the two flexed poses. Batch-local preprocessing diffuses those original labels over the neutral skin graph before the unchanged runtime falloff. This broadens the gradients without transferring heat between unconnected nearby limbs or changing other batches. The field preparation is shared by 2395 and 5266. A narrow saturated strip can still appear where a bent thigh occludes most of the abdomen; independent review distinguishes that projection from a discontinuous field. The updated exposed gradients pass bounded still review.
